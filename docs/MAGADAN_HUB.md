# Magadan: independent logistic hub investment case

## Main proposition
Assess a northern distribution center (port + storage + reefer + road delivery) **on its own revenue**. A future railway or tunnel can be an option but must never subsidize the standalone base case in the model without evidence.

## Verified starting points in research snapshots
- Port operator's reported 2025 handling: approximately 1.8347m metric tonnes; check definition and original year.
- V13 comparison found SASCO and KMP public 20ft/40ft rates Vladivostok↔Magadan. These are **not full door-to-door prices**.
- Magadan logistics center appears as 30k TEU/year in a May 2026 investor card and 150k TEU/year in October 2026 project examination news; reconcile stages and bills of quantities.
- Proposed investment card from 2026 referenced RUB 37.7bn; cannot assume same scope as 150k TEU engineering review.
- Port's published available capacity XLS from Oct 2026 has not been independently read/reconciled.
- No verified independent newly-paid contract cargo was identified in V14.

## Financial boundary
`FCFF = receipts from independently accepted cargo - operating variable costs - fixed OPEX - maintenance - capex - NWC - taxes`.

`NPV = sum(FCFF_t/(1+r)^t)`, with real/nominal WACC and timing matched.

Full regional welfare CBA must value reliability and access for residents independently of port SPV revenue, compare alternatives, and avoid double counting transfers and upstream activity.

## Incrementality
A volume moved from another terminal within the same investment group is **diverted**, not newly created. A diverted load may increase group margin if the new method saves validated costs, but cannot be called new demand.

## Minimum proof pack per pilot
1. Shipper and receiver and actual warehouses;
2. Named cargo class, gross/net container weight, Incoterms, tax treatment, equipment type;
3. Dated carrier and terminal quotes with included services;
4. Redacted historical waybills/invoices/paid proof & dated delivery actuals;
5. 24-month existing route and margin baseline, other group terminals;
6. Capacity slots and port operational availability;
7. Legal/regulatory review for the goods and counterparties;
8. Binding new demand proof or enforceable conditional purchase/transport agreement.

## Investment status
**COMMERCIAL HOLD** pending complete information. Large multi-billion RUB project and small terminal pilot should not be compared without scale and scope normalization.
