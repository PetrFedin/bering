# Rules for AI and research contributors

1. **No invented facts.** Every factual quantitative statement must cite an accessible publisher, original date, territorial perimeter, unit, and source URL or an immutable internal evidence ID. If unverified, write **NOT VERIFIED**.
2. **Do not erase history.** V1–V14 are snapshots; create a new version / commit for every material change. Do not silently revise a historical estimate to today's price base.
3. **Four investment perimeters:** regional logistics; railway to Magadan; railway to Chukotka; Bering tunnel and US/Canada network. Never sum their CAPEX or revenue without explicit reconciliation.
4. **Metrics:** actual vs planned vs terminal capacity vs observed cargo vs contract volume vs modeled cargo vs potential market are distinct types. Tons, wet tons, short tons, TEU, containers, tonne-km, RUB, USD, DKK, EUR and VAT basis must not be mixed.
5. **Zero contracts rule:** public tariffs, announcements, RFQ drafts and private guesses do **not** create bankable contract cargo. Missing price or volume = `null` / `UNVERIFIED`, not zero.
6. **Door-to-door comparisons:** same origin, destination, Incoterm, loading type, weight, week, currency, VAT, included terminal operations and equipment ownership. Prevent duplicate handling charges.
7. **Additionality:** calculate new cargo and diverted cargo separately, removing existing-group displacement and duplicates. A relocated load can add incremental profit, but cannot be relabeled "new tons."
8. **Legal and ESG:** sanctions/export controls, customs origin, environmental review, rights of Indigenous communities and cross-border permissions are required gates. No assumed Russia–USA treaty or construction approval.
9. **Financial discipline:** itemized CAPEX, OPEX, replacements, construction delay, terminal operating constraints and P50/P90 risk ranges must have data sources. Do not call assumed 5% a project WACC.
10. **Reproducibility:** spreadsheets should have source comments, editable assumptions, clear status, formula QA, SHA-256 manifest, and separate baseline and scenarios.
11. **Security and privacy:** do not commit email secrets, API keys, personal identifiers, non-public customer invoices/waybills or confidential offers. Redact and get redistribution permission first.
12. **GitHub procedure:** work in a topic branch for updates, protect `main` by pull request when possible, use descriptive commit messages, do not force push or delete source files. Record what was actually tested.
13. **Research publishing:** qualified statements only; sources support exactly the stated geographic and temporal scope, and uncertainty appears next to the claim.
14. **Current HOLD:** Bering crossing is not investable without independent engineering studies, real contracts, lawful operating regime, and full network connectivity.
